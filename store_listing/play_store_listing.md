## Full Description (max 4000 chars)
Discover, browse, and purchase your favorite books with BooksBound — a modern Flutter-based book store app built for readers who want a clean, fast, and beautiful shopping experience.

[FEATURES]
- Browse hundreds of books across 8 categories (Fiction, Non-Fiction, Children, Academic, Science, History, Biography, Religion)
- Detailed book pages with reviews, ratings, and related books
- Add books to your wishlist and cart
- Secure checkout with order tracking (Pending → Shipped → Delivered)
- Write reviews and rate books you've read
- Dark mode for comfortable night reading
- Order history with status tracking
- Forgot password flow with email reset
- Admin panel: manage books, orders, users, and reviews

[TECHNOLOGY]
Built with Flutter + Firebase. Uses Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Storage, Crashlytics, Analytics, and Remote Config.

[PRIVACY]
- No plaintext password storage
- Role-based Firestore security rules
- Firebase App Check for backend protection
- Delete your account and data anytime
