# BooksBound User Guide

This guide explains how to use the BooksBound bookstore app. The same tutorials and FAQs are available inside the app under **Profile → Help & FAQ**.

---

## 1. Getting started

### Create an account
1. Open the app and tap **Sign-up** on the login screen.
2. Enter your name, email and a password, then tap **Register**.

### Sign in
- **Email:** enter your email and password and tap **Login**.
- **Google:** tap **Continue with Google** and choose your Google account. An account is created automatically the first time.

### Forgot your password?
Tap **Forgot Password?** on the login screen, enter your email, and follow the link in the email you receive.

---

## 2. Finding books

| What you want | How |
| --- | --- |
| See popular and new books | The Home screen shows **Bestsellers**, **New Arrivals** and **All Books** |
| Browse by genre | Tap a category chip on Home, or **See All** next to Categories |
| Browse by author | Tap an author chip on Home, **See All** next to Authors, or tap the author's name on any book |
| Search | Open the **Search** tab and type a title, author or genre |
| Sort | On Home, tap the sort icon: Price (low/high), Newest Arrivals, or Popularity |

Tap any book to see its description, price, rating and reviews.

---

## 3. Buying books

1. On a book's page, tap **Add to Cart**.
2. Open the **Cart** tab. Use **+** and **−** to change quantities. The total updates instantly.
3. Tap **Checkout**.
4. Choose a **shipping address**: a saved one, a different one typed in, or **Save a new address**.
5. Choose a **payment method**: **Cash on Delivery** or a saved card (or **Add a card**).
6. Tap **Place Order**. A confirmation screen shows your order number.

---

## 4. Tracking orders

1. Go to **Profile → My Orders** to see your order history.
2. Tap an order to open its **tracking timeline**:
   **Pending → Processing → Shipped → Delivered**, with the date each stage was reached.
3. The timeline updates automatically when the store updates your order.

---

## 5. Ratings, reviews and likes

- Open a book and tap the stars to rate it.
- Tap **Write a Review** to share your opinion.
- Tap the **heart** next to a review to like it (tap again to unlike).

---

## 6. Wishlist

- Tap the heart on a book to save it to your wishlist.
- Open the **Wishlist** to view or remove books, or move them all to your cart.

---

## 7. Managing your profile

| Option (Profile tab) | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Edit Profile | Change your name; tap the camera icon on your photo to change it |
| Shipping Addresses | Add, edit, delete addresses and choose a default |
| Payment Methods | Save cards for faster checkout, choose a default, delete cards |
| My Orders | Order history and tracking |
| Change Password | Update your password (email accounts) |
| Light/Dark Mode | Switch the app theme |
| Help & FAQ | Tutorials and answers to common questions |
| Logout | Sign out of the app |
| Delete Account | Permanently delete your account and personal data |

**Card security:** only the card brand, last 4 digits, cardholder name and expiry date are saved. The full card number and CVV are never stored.

---

## 8. Admin guide

Admin accounts see an **Admin Panel** with:

| Section | What admins can do |
| --- | --- |
| Manage Books | Add, edit and delete books (title, author, genre, price, stock, cover, bestseller flag) |
| Manage Orders | View all orders and change status: Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled. Customers see each change on their tracking timeline |
| Manage Users | View user accounts and block/unblock them. Blocked users cannot sign in |
| Manage Reviews | Moderate reviews per book |
| Analytics | Store overview (books, users, orders, bestsellers) |

---

## 9. Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account to browse?**
Yes. Signing in keeps your cart, wishlist, orders and reviews saved to your account on any device.

**Which payment methods are accepted?**
Cash on Delivery, or a saved debit/credit card selected at checkout.

**How do I know where my order is?**
Profile → My Orders → tap the order to see its delivery timeline.

**Can I change quantities in my cart?**
Yes, with the + and − buttons. You cannot add more copies than are in stock.

**Why can't I log in?**
Check your email and password or use *Forgot Password?*. If you see "account has been blocked", contact the store administrator.

**Does the app work offline?**
Book covers you have viewed are cached and still display. An offline banner appears without a connection; placing orders requires internet.

**How do I delete my account?**
Profile → Delete Account. Your profile, cart, wishlist, saved addresses and cards are removed. Past orders are kept anonymously for store records.
